and caller-chosen paths are forbidden. Every frame is:

```text
ASCII("PLM15WRT") || u16_be(1) || u8(frame_type) || u8(0) ||
binding_digest_32 || nonce_32 || u64_be(payload_length) ||
sha256(payload)_32 || payload
```

There is exactly one frame per protocol step and exact EOF after the final
STATUS. HELLO/ACK/STATUS payloads are at most 65,536 bytes; artifact payload is
at most 16,777,216 bytes. Length is checked before allocation. Frame types are
`1 WRAPPER_HELLO`, `2 LEAF_HELLO`, `3 CAPABILITY_ADMIT`, `4 ARTIFACT_PAYLOAD`,
`5 PAYLOAD_ACK`, `6 WRITE_STATUS`. HELLO frames bind the 32-byte decoded launch
offer digest; later frames bind the 32-byte decoded capability digest. All
payloads except artifact bytes are exact `CJ(value)` without LF. Artifact bytes
are exactly `CJ(record) || 0x0a`. Any prefix/suffix, extra frame, wrong order,
unknown type/flag, length/hash mismatch, early EOF, duplicate, or trailing byte
rejects.

The wrapper and leaf HELLO values bind offer, nonce, observed native identity,
parent identity, executable, argv, cwd, environment, process group/Job, and
monotonic start observation. The parent compares both to OS handles before
creating the capability. The leaf emits PAYLOAD_ACK only after strict frame
validation and before filesystem mutation.

## 8. Native create-new transaction and reconciliation

For every target, the leaf verifies the live parent-controlled cancellation
channel, exact capability, one use, registered ordinal/path, absence under the
pinned no-follow parent, and byte/hash joins. It creates only the exact
same-directory temporary:

```text
temporary_basename = ".plm15-" || hex(SHA256(CJ({capability_id,target_ordinal,payload_sha256})))[0:32] || ".tmp"
```

It opens temp create-new/no-follow, writes exactly the framed artifact bytes,
flushes the file, atomically publishes by native no-replace rename, flushes the
parent directory where supported, and emits one status. The controller waits
for zero exit, independently reopens target no-follow, proves temporary absence,
compares the final native identity to the published handle, and recomputes exact
size/SHA/JCS-plus-LF bytes. It then constructs the next semantic record's prior
materialization evidence. No successful content edit is self-authenticating.

Targets are strictly sequential: SESSION_START, DECLARATION, OBSERVER_PRE,
PREIMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW, OBSERVER_COMMIT, PREIMPLEMENTATION_ADMISSION, then
TRANSACTION_CLOSURE. Declaration must be stably reopened before the observer
performs a fresh post-declaration enumeration used for PRE. Each target has a
new child chain, nonce, offer, capability, and target-only payload. One opaque
patch or one capability may not create two targets.

The same lease controller/session/root/lease remains live through admission
reopen and closure reopen. Only after closure confirms admission bytes and all
six preceding materializations may the controller release the lease and STOP.
Closure has every authority false and cannot authorize downstream work.

Crash rules are exact:

- before HELLO or before temp creation, with controller lease live and target
  absent, retry uses a new nonce/offer/capability;
- partial or mismatched temp is never authoritative and is moved create-new/
  no-replace to `.plm15-quarantine-<temp_native_identity_hash>-<content_hash>`;
- valid unpublished temp may reconcile only by exact capability, temp native
  identity, target absence, and bytes; never by newest/name alone;
- after rename with lost status, adoption requires exact target native identity,
  bytes, capability, child exit evidence, and parent reopen; ambiguity becomes
  immutable invalid debt;
- collision, replacement, reparse/symlink, nonzero exit, malformed/extra status,
  chain drift, lease loss, or child survival after controller death cannot PASS;
- controller or lease loss before closure preserves every visible byte and uses
  the typed successor-attempt rule; no in-place resume; and
- unsupported required primitive returns `INDETERMINATE_NO_AUTHORITY`.

Quarantine never overwrites, deletes, or becomes candidate evidence. A writer
capability expires on first ACK, target collision, controller/lease death,
protocol error, or timeout and can never be reused.
